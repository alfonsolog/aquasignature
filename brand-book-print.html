Aqua Signature · 2026 01 / Our Story
— Our Story

Born from the Caribbean.
Built for it.

Aqua Signature was founded in the Dominican Republic with one clear purpose: to bring premium outdoor living products to the vibrant Caribbean lifestyle.

We saw a gap — beautiful poolside spaces deserved accessories as elegant as their surroundings. Our answer was a curated collection of poly rattan pieces engineered for the tropics: resistant to sun, water, salt, and humidity — without sacrificing style.

We are not just selling products. We are selling an experience — the feeling of effortless, elevated Caribbean living.

"We don't sell furniture. We sell the feeling of a Tuesday afternoon at the pool."

— Maud Letellier, Founder
